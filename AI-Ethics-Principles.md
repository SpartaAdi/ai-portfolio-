# My AI Ethics Principles

## Where This Came From
I watched people share deep, unprocessed emotions with AI — and watched 
the AI validate them confidently, filling in details it was never given. 
The person, already in an emotional state, followed the output rather 
than questioning it. I tried it myself, noticed the same pattern — AI 
assuming context I hadn't shared, me going along with it — and stopped. 
I switched to journalling for anything emotionally significant. That 
experience made me realise: AI's tendency to validate and assume is a 
feature in the wrong context. It can create a vicious circle, not clarity. 
That's when I started thinking seriously about what should and shouldn't 
go into these tools.

## What AI Is For in My Work
AI is a tool for accelerating quality thinking — research, synthesis, 
stress-testing ideas, and improving outputs. It amplifies judgment. 
It does not replace it. I am responsible for everything that leaves me.

## Data I Never Input
- Client name combined with deal terms, pricing, or proposal context 
  into consumer-tier tools — enterprise Claude only
- Internal margin, bid strategy, or AWS pricing/incentive program data
- Prospect proposal or pipeline data outside Minfy enterprise license
- AWS partner funding or commercial incentive details
- Colleague performance, HR-adjacent, or personal information about others
- Annual deal closure data or sales performance metrics into 
  consumer-tier tools (corrected — previously used Claude Pro for 
  formatting; moved to enterprise Claude)
- My own deep emotional state or personal crisis — journalling instead

## How I Handle AI Outputs
- All AI-drafted client-facing content is reviewed before sending
- Factual claims are verified against primary sources before use
- I disclose AI use to stakeholders when asked directly
- I do not submit AI-generated work as entirely my own in formal contexts
- When AI validates an emotional position confidently, I treat that 
  as a signal to step back — not go further

## AI Tier Awareness
| Tier | Tools I use | Data risk | My use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer | Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, Gemini 3.1 Pro (web) | May be used for training — assume it is | Personal queries, non-sensitive exploration, email cleanup (no client context) |
| API | Gemini API (GCP personal account) | Not used for training by default | Powers product queries — public information only |
| Enterprise | Claude Enterprise (Minfy license) | Contractual guarantee — no training on data | All work tasks: client context, proposals, deal data, internal comms |

## What I Will Not Do
- Paste client names, deal sizes, or commercial terms into a 
  consumer-tier tool — even for formatting or cleanup
- Follow AI output on an emotionally charged topic without 
  cross-checking it against my own thinking first
- Use AI-generated content for financial or legal decisions at 
  work without expert review
- Bypass a client's stated AI usage policy on any engagement
- Use Minfy's enterprise license for personal projects without 
  confirming it is within company policy
